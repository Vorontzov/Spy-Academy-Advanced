        SPY ACADEMY / FIELD MANUAL

        WELCOME TO ADVANCED OPERATIONS.

        Recover every marked mission item, then reach extraction on floor 01. Every case has a maths lock, starting in operation one. Action pauses while you think. Wrong answers never cost lives. Guided help requires a correct input at each step; your working is saved.

        01. Find a route

        Move with A/D or ←/→. W/S or ↑/↓ call and ride lifts. At a stair marker, ↑/↓ uses the staircase. E or ↑ opens a case. Space jumps. Wait for a lift platform before crossing its shaft.

        02. Choose your equipment

        X fires. Q cycles a rapid pulse pistol, a three-shot scatter blaster and an explosive arc launcher. C performs a close-range takedown. All weapons stun guards. Armored guards require two ordinary hits; an EMP or takedown bypasses their armor.

        03. Use spy gadgets

        V chooses cloak, EMP or grapple; G activates it. Cloak hides you for six seconds. EMP stuns nearby guards and clears pulses. Grapple pulls you up one floor on solid ground. Gadgets spend rechargeable energy. Pick up batteries, shields, medkits and gold intel.

        04. Escape with the evidence

        After collecting all items, reach floor 01 extraction to begin a car, motorcycle, helicopter, plane or parachute stage. Steer with arrows or WASD; X clears hazards, G shields, Space boosts (except while parachuting). Collect beacons and dodge obstacles until the route ends. Pausing works in both phases. Continue restores lives without losing recovered items or escape distance.

        Car escapes pause twice at a fuel lab: move the valve to split amber and blue fuel. Motorcycle escapes pause at dispatch junctions: steer to the calculated exit and press FIRE. Correct engineering earns a six-second boost. Help requires a checked input for each calculation; unfinished vehicle tasks are saved.

        Phantom Circuit (operations 37–60) introduces eight new settings and towers up to 30 floors. Each operation mixes its new skill with prerequisite review: build confidence, apply the skill, then solve its advanced form. Select a chapter in the operation menu.

        Shadow Atlas (operations 25–36) adds weapon caches, chained objectives and named villains. Two missions start unarmed; C or FIRE performs a takedown until you find a cache. Ion rails pierce guards and use 8 energy; ricochet discs return from a wall and use 6. Override the defenses to stop floor traps and remove the villain’s shield. Jump or wait while a trap warns, and use the villain’s recovery window. On Arctic floors, allow room to stop on the ice. Defeat the villain before the final transmission.
