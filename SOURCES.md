# Supplied source collection

The source collection now contains 38 individual PDFs (243 pages): the earlier 24 files plus 14 supplied for Phantom Circuit. The original ZIP exceeded the transfer tool’s 32 MiB limit. All 38 individual PDFs were downloaded and their pages inventoried. Text was extracted from text PDFs; scanned files and the corrupted polynomial-basics text were OCRed. **This is a curriculum mapping, not a claim that every problem or answer in all files has been individually read, verified or imported.** Relevant sections were inspected to author the current generators. Some OCR is imperfect, especially stacked fractions, minus signs and exponents; uncertain expressions were not copied into the game.

**Re-upload check:** all 13 PDFs supplied for the Shadow Atlas expansion match the SHA-256 hashes of previously supplied files. The earlier game used selected skills from 11, held S19 for a later expansion, and catalogued S18 as an answer companion. Shadow Atlas now uses S19 and adds new families from the existing source collection; it does not create duplicate source records.

PDF page numbers below are one-based unless marked “printed.” Source PDFs and OCR working files are not published in this repository. New generated questions retain source IDs and locations; their answers and intermediate calculations are independently checked by the test suite. [source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json) records original filenames, page counts and file hashes for future matching.

## Editorial decisions

- Problems are algorithmic adaptations of the source skills, with new numbers and contexts. The campaign is not the complete SHSAT paper or a complete test-preparation syllabus.
- A source answer key supports review but is not treated as infallible. The inverse-variation ice example (S22, problem 3) was excluded. Generated inverse-variation problems explicitly state the fixed quantity: supplies, travel distance, or teeth passing per minute.
- Fraction simplification guides accept equivalent values while showing the simplest form after completion. Polynomial outputs omit zero terms and unnecessary coefficients of one.
- Vehicle dispatch/fuel tasks are additional adaptations of S06 fraction products, S14 percent-of-a-whole, S17 sharing ratios, and S09/S21 linear equations with constant rates. The interception story and interactive fuel lab are original contexts, not quoted source exercises.
- Source directions such as “solve each equation” or “circle the answer” describe the worksheet task; they do not override the user's game requirements.
- No extra files or contents are inferred from the inaccessible ZIP. This manifest covers the 38 successfully provided individual PDFs only.
